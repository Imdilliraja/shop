About Dilliraja Shop

Our Story

Dilliraja Shop was established in 2014 in the heart of Arni, Tamil Nadu. What started as a small mobile phone retail store has now grown into one of the most trusted names in the local mobile market. Our journey began with a simple vision - to provide genuine mobile phones and accessories at the best prices to our valued customers.

Over the years, we have served thousands of happy customers, helping them choose the perfect mobile phone that suits their needs and budget. Our commitment to quality and customer satisfaction has made us a household name in Arni and surrounding areas.

Our Mission

To be Arni's most trusted mobile phone destination by offering genuine products, competitive prices, and exceptional customer service. We aim to bridge the gap between online and offline shopping by providing the convenience of digital platforms with the trust of a local store.

  • Provide 100% genuine products with manufacturer warranty
  • Offer the best prices in the market
  • Deliver exceptional after-sales support
  • Help customers make informed purchasing decisions
  • Build long-lasting relationships based on trust

Why Choose Us?

  • 10+ Years of Trust: Serving Arni since 2014 with integrity and honesty.
  • Genuine Products: All products sourced directly from authorized distributors.
  • Best Price Guarantee: We match any price you find elsewhere.
  • Expert Guidance: Our knowledgeable staff helps you choose the right phone.
  • After-Sales Support: We're here to help even after you make a purchase.
  • Exchange Offers: Get the best value for your old phone.

Our Values

At Dilliraja Shop, we believe in:

  • Transparency: No hidden charges, no surprises.
  • Quality: Only genuine, warranty-backed products.
  • Customer First: Your satisfaction is our success.
  • Integrity: Honest business practices at all times.
  • Innovation: Constantly improving to serve you better.
